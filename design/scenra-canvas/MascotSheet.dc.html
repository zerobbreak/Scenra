SCENRA MASCOT

Meet Cue

A little prompter with a cursor for an antenna. Cue lives in the pill, reads along with you and shows what Scenra is about to do, so you can glance without reading.

  • Red tally light = OBS is recording
  • Never appears in the video
  • Motions under 400 ms, still with Reduce motion
  • Can be switched off in Settings

Ready

During the countdown. Bobs gently, smiling.

Reading along

While you speak. Eyes track your line, mouth moves with your voice.

About to act

An action is a few words away. Leans in, points, cursor glows.

Clicking

On the trigger word. A quick squash and a ripple from the cursor.

Puzzled

Target not found. Turns amber, scratches its head and waits for you.

Relieved

You approved a fix. A check pops, then back to reading.

Celebrating

Scene kept. One hop and a few sparkles, then settles.

Dozing

Paused for a while. Wakes up on your first word.
